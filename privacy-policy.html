Traceless Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Summary

Traceless performs scanning and cleaning on your Mac. It does not upload your files, paths, scan results or folder contents to a server.

Data processed on your Mac

When you authorize a folder, Traceless reads file names, sizes, modification dates and local metadata to identify reclaimable files and explain the impact of cleaning. Folder access is controlled by macOS App Sandbox and your selections.

Data collection

Traceless does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell or share personal data.

Storage and deletion

Traceless stores only the local folder authorization bookmark needed to provide the requested features. You can revoke folder access from Settings. Files moved to the Trash remain subject to macOS Trash behavior.

Contact

For questions, visit our Support page.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
